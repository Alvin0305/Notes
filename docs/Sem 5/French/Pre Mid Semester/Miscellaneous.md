#### "to be" (etre)

- I am -> Je sius
- You are -> Tu es (singular informal)
- He/She/It is -> Il/Elle/on est
- We are -> Nous sommes
- You are -> Vous êtes
- They are -> Ils/Elles sont

#### "to have" (avoir)

- I have -> J'ai
- You have -> Tu as
- He/She have -> Il/Elle a
- We have -> Nous avons
- You have -> Vous avez
- They have -> Ils/Elles ont
